User Manual

1. Getting Started

  • Importing Files: Click or drag-and-drop into Choose File for single images, or Choose Folder to ingest an entire directory.
  • Removing Images: Hover over any thumbnail in the bottom strip and click the ✕ icon to remove it.
  • Apply Target: Selecting All Images instantly clones your slider settings, white balance, and crop coordinates across the entire batch queue.

2. Memory Presets

  • Save: Click to store your exact current slider adjustments and white balance math securely in your browser's local memory.
  • Load Settings: Applies your saved preset instantly. Combined with "All Images", this is the fastest way to process a new batch of documents.

3. White Balance (Color Neutralization)

  • Pick White (Eyedropper): Click this tool, then click any area of the image that should be pure white. The engine averages a 5x5 pixel grid around your click to calculate the perfect offset.
  • Auto Correct: Mathematically analyzes the image to automatically strip away blue or yellow color casts.
  • Color Channels: If the eyedropper pushes a dark spot too far into the red spectrum, use the new Red/Green/Blue channel sliders to manually pull back the specific blowout.

4. Geometry (Deskew & Cropping)

  • Perspective Crop: Drag the corner handles to match a skewed document. Click Apply Crop to warp the selection flat.
  • Auto Deskew: Automatically straightens crooked scans up to ±12 degrees.

5. Enhancements & Adjustments

  • Auto Levels: Instantly snaps brightness and contrast to optimal legibility levels.
  • Auto Sharpen: Applies a 25% calculated matrix to enhance edge contrast for faded ink.
  • Manual Sliders: Fine-tune Sharpen, Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Exposure, Temperature, and Tint.

6. Exporting the Batch

  • Format Selection: Choose between JPEG, WEBP, and PNG.
  • Resize Settings: Check "Do Not Resize" to export raw files, or use the custom width/height tools.
  • Export Batch: Compresses the processed queue into a single downloadable ZIP archive.
